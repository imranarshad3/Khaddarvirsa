# AI model photo prompts — Khaddar Virsa

Use these with Gemini (gemini.google.com, image generation) or any similar image tool.

## How to use

1. Open the product's main photo from the site (or from your own originals) and **attach it to the prompt** as a reference image. This is the most important step: it makes the tool copy your real weave and colour.
2. Paste the **shared style** block, then the product's prompt below it.
3. Ask for **portrait 4:5** (product pages, cards) and once more in **landscape 3:2** (banners).
4. Generate 3–4 versions. Keep the one where the fabric colour and texture match your photo most closely; check hands, buttons and collar for AI mistakes.
5. Send me the files you like. I'll crop, warm-grade, compress and place them on the site.

## Shared style (paste first every time)

```
Premium fashion campaign photograph for a Pakistani menswear brand.
A fictional Pakistani man in his late 20s to 30s with natural skin texture, neat beard or clean-shaven, confident relaxed expression, not a celebrity and not resembling any real person.
He wears a tailored stitched shalwar kameez made from the fabric shown in the reference image: match its exact colour, weave texture and slub pattern. Classic kameez with a band collar, side slits, button placket, matching shalwar, black leather peshawari chappal.
Warm natural golden-hour light, soft shadows, shallow depth of field, shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm lens, editorial magazine quality, true-to-life colours.
No text, no logos, no labels, no price tags, no watermark. Full body or three-quarter framing with space on one side for website text.
```

## Rules to keep it safe

- Never name a real actor, model or influencer in a prompt, and don't use a real person's photo as the reference.
- Keep your real fabric photo as the first product image. AI photos go after it, so buyers always see the true fabric.
- If a generated photo shows the fabric in a clearly different colour, don't use it.
